Magazine: Communication of the ACM (HKUST)

Observations:
1. Most AI-generated poems are too mechanical
	- overuse of cliched metaphors (oceans of sorrow/soul of fire)
	- lacking fluidity
	- tonal restraint

2. Lack of well-defined metrics for AI-generated creative writings evaluation
	- current measure: fluency, coherence ....
	- lacking: cultural context, rhythm, ambiguity, [^1]phonetic texture

3. The definition of creative writing
	- Varies across stylistic, phonological and cultural dimension
	- AI need to be trained for specific genres, traditions, periods to ensure alignment with their expressive convention

Concepts:
1. BLEU (Bilingual Evaluation Understudy)
	- automated metric used to evaluate machine-translated text by comparing model-generated output against one or more human reference translations
